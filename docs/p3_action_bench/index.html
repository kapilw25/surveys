Do world models make better robots? A survey of benchmarks for predictive embodied intelligence.

Central question: across the robot-evaluation landscape, which benchmarks can actually establish that predictive world modeling produces a measurable, closed-loop behavioral advantage over direct Vision-Language-Action policies, and for which robotic capabilities?
