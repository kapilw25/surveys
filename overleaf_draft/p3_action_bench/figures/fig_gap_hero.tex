\caption{\textbf{The question no benchmark answers.} The robot-evaluation landscape splits into three
lanes: \emph{open-loop} suites (43) score a world model's prediction or generation quality but never
execute it; \emph{closed-loop} suites (113) score task success but are model-agnostic and never
build a direct Vision-Language-Action vs world-model contrast; and a handful of \emph{bridges} (4)
reach executed action yet omit the per-capability head-to-head. The empty cell each lane points to,
a per-capability contrast that isolates whether prediction earns a closed-loop advantage, is built
explicitly by only \textbf{11 of 160} benchmarks (7\%). This survey maps the landscape by that
missing axis (Table~\ref{tab:landscape}).}

VLA-versus-world-model contrast built explicitly by only 11 of 160 benchmarks. Arrow labels read
``visual quality is not task success'', ``model-agnostic, no prediction ablation'', and ``no
per-capability VLA head-to-head''.}
